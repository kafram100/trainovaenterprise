# PROJECT TITLE

Trainova Enterprise Digital Education Ecosystem

---

# PROJECT OVERVIEW

Trainova Enterprise is a modern educational technology ecosystem focused on building intelligent digital infrastructure for educational institutions across Africa.

The ecosystem combines:

* school infrastructure systems
* AI-powered assessment systems
* timetable automation
* educational media
* practical digital skills training
* student-focused educational resources

The platform is designed to modernize educational administration, assessments, communication, learning, and institutional productivity.

The system must feel:

* modern
* minimal
* scalable
* professional
* educational
* intelligent
* trustworthy

Trainova is not just building software.

Trainova is building:

> “The digital infrastructure powering the future of education in Africa.”
